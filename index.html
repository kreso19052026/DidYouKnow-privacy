Privacy Policy — Did You Know? Trivia Last updated: 3 October 2026 This policy explains how the Android app Did You Know? Trivia, developed by Kozo Technology, handles your information. Data Stored on Your Device All game data (scores, XP, level, streaks, achievements, daily missions, player names you enter and settings) is stored locally on your device only. The app does not send this data to us or to any server we run. If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your own device backup. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Account No account registration is required to use this app. Advertising and Your Consent This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads. The app works fully whatever you choose. United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising. Change your choice at any time: open the app's Settings → Privacy Options. This entry appears whenever it applies to your region. Notifications The app may ask for permission to send a daily reminder about the Daily Challenge. Reminders are created on your device; no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings. Children This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Changes to This Policy If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
